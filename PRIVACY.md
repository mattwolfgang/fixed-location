# Fixed Location privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Fixed Location stores the location you type into its popup (latitude,
longitude, accuracy) and whether it is switched on. These settings are kept in
your browser's local extension storage on your own device.

When the extension is switched on and a website asks your browser for your
location through the Geolocation API, the extension answers with the location
you set, in place of the browser's own. The website receives it just as it
would receive a location from the browser, and the website's own privacy policy
then applies to it. When the extension is switched off, it passes the request
to the browser unchanged.

The extension does not send your location, browsing activity or any other data
to the developer or any other party. It has no analytics, no tracking and no
remote servers, and it does not load remote code.

You can remove the stored settings at any time by uninstalling the extension.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/mattwolfgang/fixed-location/issues
